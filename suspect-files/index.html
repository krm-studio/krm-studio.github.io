Suspect Files

A detective puzzle game for Android.

Read the case, check the alibis, unseal the evidence and accuse the culprit.

  • 81 cases across 6 chapters, each with its own suspects, motives and alibis.
  • 7 languages: English, العربية, Français, Español, Deutsch, Italiano, Português.
  • Plays offline. An account is optional.
