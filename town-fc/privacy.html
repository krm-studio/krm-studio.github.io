1. Who we are

Town FC: Club Owner Tycoon (“the game”) is a football club owner game developed by KRM Studio (“we”, “us”). This policy explains what information the game handles, why, and the choices you have.

2. The short version

  • You can play offline as a guest. Your career then stays on your phone.
  • If you play online or create an account, we store an account identifier, your club details and your online results.
  • Payments are handled by Google Play and processed through RevenueCat. We never see your card details.
  • The game shows ads from Google AdMob, which may use your advertising ID. You choose in the consent form.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • You can delete your account and online data at any time from inside the game.

3. Information the game handles

3.1 On your device

Your career save (club, town, squad, finances, seasons), your coin wallet, your language and settings. This is stored on your device and removed when you uninstall the game.

3.2 Account information (optional)

When you go online an anonymous account identifier is created. If you register with email we store your email address; your password is held only in hashed form by our authentication provider, Supabase. If you sign in with Google we receive your name, email address and Google account identifier from Google.

3.3 Cloud backup (signed-in players)

If you sign in with email or Google, a backup of your career save and coin wallet is stored with Supabase so you can restore your club on another phone. It is updated automatically after matchdays. It is deleted when you delete your account. Guests are never backed up.

3.4 Owner profile

The nationality you choose for your owner profile is stored on your device and, if you sign in, in your backup. It is only used to personalise the game.

3.5 Online play

For online leagues, cups and rankings we store your club name, club strength, owner level, friend code, referral code and match results. Other players see your club name, rating and friend code, never your email address.

3.6 Purchases

Purchases are made through Google Play. RevenueCat receives the purchase record (product, time, price, an anonymous or account identifier and the Google Play purchase token) to confirm the purchase is valid and deliver it to you.

3.7 Ads

Google AdMob may collect your advertising ID, approximate location derived from your IP address and ad interactions to show and measure ads. In the European Economic Area and the UK you are asked for consent first. You can reset your advertising ID in your device settings.

4. How we use information

To run your account, online matches, cups, rankings and friend invites; to confirm and deliver purchases; to show ads; to prevent cheating and abuse; and to fix problems and improve the game.

5. Who we share it with

ProviderPurpose
SupabaseAccounts, sign-in and online game data (hosted in the EU)

We share information only with these service providers, or when the law requires it. We do not sell personal information.

6. Children

Town FC is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 where local law requires). If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

7. Your choices and rights

  • Delete your account and online data in the game: Profile → Delete account, or see how to delete your account.
  • Ask us for a copy of your data, or to correct it, at supportkrmstudio@gmail.com.
  • Change your ad consent from the game settings, and reset your advertising ID in Android settings.

8. Keeping and protecting data

Online data is kept while your account exists and deleted when you delete the account; residual backup copies are removed within 30 days. Data travels encrypted over HTTPS.

9. Changes

We may update this policy. We will change the effective date above and tell you in the game about important changes.

10. Contact

KRM Studio — supportkrmstudio@gmail.com

2. باختصار

  • يمكنك اللعب دون اتصال كضيف، وتبقى مسيرتك على هاتفك.
  • إذا لعبت عبر الإنترنت أو أنشأت حساباً نخزّن معرّف الحساب وبيانات ناديك ونتائجك على الإنترنت.
  • المدفوعات تتم عبر Google Play وتُعالَج من خلال RevenueCat. لا نرى بيانات بطاقتك أبداً.
  • تعرض اللعبة إعلانات Google AdMob التي قد تستخدم معرّف الإعلانات. أنت تختار في نموذج الموافقة.
  • لا نبيع معلوماتك الشخصية.
  • يمكنك حذف حسابك وبياناتك على الإنترنت في أي وقت من داخل اللعبة.

3. المعلومات التي تتعامل معها اللعبة

3.1 على جهازك

حفظ مسيرتك (النادي والمدينة والفريق والأموال والمواسم) ومحفظة العملات واللغة والإعدادات. تُحفظ على جهازك وتُحذف عند إزالة اللعبة.

3.2 بيانات الحساب (اختياري)

عند اللعب عبر الإنترنت يُنشأ معرّف حساب مجهول. إذا سجّلت بالبريد نخزّن بريدك الإلكتروني، وتُحفظ كلمة المرور بصيغة مشفّرة فقط لدى مزوّد المصادقة Supabase. إذا سجّلت الدخول بحساب Google نستلم من جوجل اسمك وبريدك ومعرّف حسابك.

3.3 اللعب عبر الإنترنت

للدوريات والكؤوس والتصنيفات على الإنترنت نخزّن اسم ناديك وقوته ومستوى المالك ورمز الصديق ورمز الدعوة ونتائج المباريات. يرى اللاعبون الآخرون اسم ناديك وتقييمه ورمز الصديق، ولا يرون بريدك أبداً.

3.4 المشتريات

تتم المشتريات عبر Google Play. يستلم RevenueCat سجل الشراء (المنتج والوقت والسعر ومعرّف مجهول أو معرّف الحساب ورمز الشراء من Google Play) للتأكد من صحة الشراء وتسليمه لك.

3.5 الإعلانات

قد يجمع Google AdMob معرّف الإعلانات والموقع التقريبي المستنتج من عنوان IP والتفاعل مع الإعلانات لعرضها وقياسها. في المنطقة الاقتصادية الأوروبية والمملكة المتحدة نطلب موافقتك أولاً. يمكنك إعادة تعيين معرّف الإعلانات من إعدادات جهازك.
